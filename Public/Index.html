Dear Saniya,

I don't know what every day feels like inside your heart, and I won't pretend that I do. But I hope you always remember that you don't have to carry every little weight completely alone.

You are allowed to have quiet days. You are allowed to feel tired, uncertain, or simply not like yourself. You don't have to turn every sadness into a smile just to make the world around you comfortable.

If today has been heavy, let this be your gentle reminder: one hard moment does not define your story. You are still growing, still learning, and still endlessly worthy of softness and kindness—even on the days when you cannot see your own light.

Whenever the world gets too loud, come back here. Let the sky slow down, let the stars stay with you, and remember that someone is always wishing you gentle moments.

With care & warmth, Rudra ♡
